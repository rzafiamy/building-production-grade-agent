## Dedication

*To my wife —*
*for every evening you held things together while I stayed at the screen.*
*For every weekend you gave up so I could learn one more thing.*
*For the patience that never ran out, even when mine did.*
*This book exists because you made space for it.*

*To my daughter and my son —*
*for the nights you went to bed without me there to say goodnight,*
*and still came running in the morning with a smile.*
*You are the reason everything I build matters.*

*And to God —*
*for the love that has followed me since 2023,*
*through every late night, every failed experiment, every breakthrough.*
*Every line of code that finally worked was a small grace.*
*Thank you.*

---

*This journey started in 2023 with a question: can software think for itself?*
*Three years later, the answer is still incomplete — but the pursuit has been worth it.*
*Whatever is good in this book, I owe to the people and the grace that carried me here.*
