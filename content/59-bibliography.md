### Citation Guidelines for Writers

When adding citations:
1. Use format: Author(s) (Year). *Title*. Publication.
2. For papers: include conference or journal if known.
3. For blog posts: cite by author and approximate year only (no URLs).
4. For books: include publisher and edition if relevant.
5. Add to this list alphabetically by first author's last name.
